Solar light. Smart tablets. A real chance to read.

Who We Are

A team of educators, engineers, and designers building accessible literacy technology for children who have never had the chance to attend a well-resourced school.

🛠️

What We Do

We install solar-powered "smart library" sheds with AI tutoring tablets in high-population, low-resource communities — starting in Niger.

💡

The Value We Offer

Personalized, gamified literacy lessons that adapt to each child, plus real-time monitoring so every device stays powered, safe, and online.

🔎

Why Look Closer

Low cost per child, offline-capable design, and built-in security make this one of the most scalable literacy models for hard-to-reach regions.

The Problem

Learning poverty starts a lifelong cycle.

UNICEF reports that 93% of students in primary grades 2 through 5 in low-income regions cannot read or do mathematics properly. In Niger, the crisis is even deeper — among the worst literacy rates in the world.

📚

Struggling in every subject

Reading is the foundation for math, science, history, and beyond.

🎓

Less likely to continue school

Falling behind early makes it harder to keep up as lessons advance.

🔄

The cycle repeats

Low literacy limits parents' ability to support the next generation's education.

Our Innovation

A solar-powered smart library that teaches kids to read — with or without the grid.

Generative AI tutoring, machine-learning personalization, and IoT-monitored solar power come together in one rugged, theft-protected library shed.
