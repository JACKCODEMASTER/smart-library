# Brand Basics

## Business Name
**Smart Library**

## Tagline
*Solar light. Smart tablets. A real chance to read.*

## One-Sentence Positioning
A marketing site for **Smart Library**, a solar-powered smart-library network that helps **children in low-literacy regions of Niger** learn to read through AI-driven, personalized, gamified lessons — even without electricity or internet.

## Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / Sun | Solar Orange | `#F4A300` |
| Primary Dark | Deep Orange | `#D98500` |
| Secondary / Sky | Deep Night Blue | `#0B3954` |
| Secondary Light | Lake Blue | `#145374` |
| Background | Warm Sand | `#FDF6EC` |
| Background Alt | Sand Dark | `#F3E8D6` |
| Accent / Growth | Green | `#2E8B57` |
| Alert / Problem | Red | `#C94C4C` |
| Text | Charcoal | `#1F2937` |

**Logic:** Orange/sun = solar power + hope/light. Deep blue = technology, trust, "night sky" the solar panel charges under. Green = growth and positive outcomes. Red is used sparingly, only for "the problem" statistics, to create emotional contrast with the hopeful solution colors.

## Typography
- **Headings:** Poppins (600–800 weight) — confident, modern, slightly rounded geometric sans. Feels technical but approachable, not corporate-cold.
- **Body:** Nunito Sans (400–700) — highly legible at small sizes, warm and friendly without being childish.

## Tone of Voice
**Mission-driven + credible technical.** Not playful/cartoonish (this is the adult-facing "about us" / partnerships site, not the kids' app). Not overly corporate either. Think: a serious nonprofit/edtech pitch deck — direct, data-backed, human. Sentences lead with the child's reality, back it up with a statistic, then pivot to the engineered solution.

## Logo / Icon
Simple circular sun mark with a stylized open-book/awning silhouette inside (`<svg>` inline logo used in the nav and footer across every page — see `features.html` or `index.html` header). Chosen so it reads clearly at 34px in the nav bar and scales down to a favicon-size mark without losing meaning: *sun (solar power) over a shelter (the library shed)*.
