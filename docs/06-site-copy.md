## Home (`index.html`)
**Eyebrow:** A Niger Literacy Initiative
**Headline:** Solar light. Smart tablets. A real chance to read.
**Value proposition:** Smart Library builds solar-powered smart libraries that bring AI-driven, personalized literacy lessons to children in Niger — where up to 90% of kids reach the end of primary school unable to read a simple sentence.
**Primary CTAs:** "Learn Our Story" → About Us · "Partner With Us" → Get Involved
**Headline stats:** 90% of kids in Niger are living in poverty · 93% of kids in grades 2–5 can't read to their grade level*

**Clear Message grid:**
- *Who We Are* — A team of educators, engineers, and designers building accessible literacy technology for children who have never had the chance to attend a well-resourced school.
- *What We Do* — We install solar-powered "smart library" sheds with AI tutoring tablets in high-population, low-resource communities — starting in Niger.
- *The Value We Offer* — Personalized, gamified literacy lessons that adapt to each child, plus real-time monitoring so every device stays powered, safe, and online.
- *Why Look Closer* — Low cost per child, offline-capable design, and built-in security make this one of the most scalable literacy models for hard-to-reach regions.

**The Problem:** UNICEF reports that 93% of students in primary grades 2 through 5 in low-income regions cannot read or do mathematics properly. In Niger, the crisis is even deeper — among the worst literacy rates in the world. (Struggling in every subject → Less likely to continue school → The cycle repeats.)

**Our Innovation teaser:** A solar-powered smart library that teaches kids to read — with or without the grid. Generative AI tutoring, machine-learning personalization, and IoT-monitored solar power come together in one rugged, theft-protected library shed. CTA: "See How It Works" → Features.

**Closing CTA:** Help us light the way to literacy. Whether you're a local government, an NGO like UNICEF, or a company interested in education data partnerships — there's a role for you.

## Features (`features.html`)
**Headline:** Every Feature, Built for One Goal: Reading.
**Value proposition:** Each feature removes one real barrier standing between a child in Niger and the ability to read — powered by generative AI, machine learning, and the Internet of Things.
- **Feature 01 — Generative AI:** An AI tutor that writes lessons in the child's own language. *Why it matters:* gives children who've never had a personal tutor one, in the language they already speak.
- **Feature 02 — Machine Learning:** Lessons that adapt the moment a child struggles or succeeds. *Why it matters:* personalization at Lexia-level quality, at a fraction of the cost, on shared hardware.
- **Feature 03 — Internet of Things:** Sensors that keep every library powered, safe, and online. *Why it matters:* lets a small team remotely manage many unstaffed rural sites.
- **Closing table:** maps every feature → its 4IR technology → the specific barrier it removes.

## Where We Work (`where-we-work.html`)
**Headline:** Starting in Niger.
**Why Niger:** Niger has some of the lowest literacy rates in the world, with approximately 90% of children in learning poverty. Combined with high population density, it's the right place to prove this model works.
**Pilot regions:** Maradi Region, Zinder Region, Dosso Region — each with a one-line rationale.
**Selection criteria:** high population/low resources, reliable solar exposure, mobile network coverage, community safety & buy-in.
**Expansion plan:** Niger is the beginning, not the end.
