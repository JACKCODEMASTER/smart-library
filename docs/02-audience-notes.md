# Audience Notes

This site is explicitly **not** built for the children who use the tablets — it is the institutional "About Us" / partnership site for the adults who fund, approve, and operate the program.

## Primary Audience Segments

### 1. Local & National Government — Education Ministries
- **Cares about:** cost per child, scalability, security of hardware/data, alignment with national curriculum and language policy, proof the model won't embarrass or burden local officials.
- **Wants to do on the site:** understand the hardware/security model quickly (Features page), see which exact regions are targeted (Where We Work), find a direct contact path (Contact / Get Involved).

### 2. Donor Organizations (e.g. UNICEF, foundations, NGOs)
- **Cares about:** evidence of need (learning-poverty statistics), a credible theory of change, measurable outcomes, how funding translates into concrete units (tablets, sheds, regions).
- **Wants to do on the site:** see the problem quantified, see funding tiers and what each dollar amount buys, read testimonials/credibility signals, reach partnerships contact.

### 3. Corporate / Data Partners
- **Cares about:** ethical data handling, anonymization, opt-in consent, what insight they'd actually get.
- **Wants to do on the site:** find the dedicated "Responsible Data Partnerships" section (Get Involved page) and see it's consent-based and anonymized before considering outreach.

### 4. Individual Donors / General Public / Press
- **Cares about:** an emotionally clear story — who is affected, why, and what their money changes.
- **Wants to do on the site:** read Our Story / Why We Created This, see real stat-backed impact, click Donate.

## What We Want Every Visitor To Do
Primary CTA across the site: **move toward a conversation or a contribution** — either "Support a Library" (donate) or "Partner With Us" (contact form / partnerships page). Every page funnels toward `get-involved.html` or `contact.html`.

## Target Market Signals in Copy & Imagery
- Headline stats (90% poverty, 93% can't read to grade level) are Niger-specific and sourced to UNICEF, signaling credibility to institutional funders.
- Real photographs of Nigerien children (home page) make the beneficiary population visually unmistakable — this is not a generic global edtech pitch, it is Niger-first.
- Named pilot regions (Maradi, Zinder, Dosso) signal operational seriousness to government/NGO readers who know the geography.
- The Features page's "Barrier It Removes" table speaks directly to funders evaluating ROI/impact per dollar.
