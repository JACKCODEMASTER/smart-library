# Sitemap

The site was deliberately scoped down to only the pages tied directly to the grading rubric, plus a page to make the target market explicit.

| Page | File | What Belongs On It |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | `index.html` | Hero with one-sentence value proposition + primary CTA; two real photos of Nigerien children flanking the two headline statistics (90% poverty / 93% can't read to grade level); "Who We Are / What We Do / Value We Offer / Why Look Closer" clear-message grid; "The Problem" section (learning poverty cycle); one-line "Our Innovation" teaser linking to Features; regional/impact stats band; a self-contained "Get Involved" CTA section (`#get-involved`) with a direct email CTA, since there is no separate Get Involved/Contact page. |
| **Features** | `features.html` | Each major feature (Generative AI tutor, Machine-learning personalization, IoT monitoring) shown with a concept-rendering image, a plain-language explanation, and a "Why it matters" line tying it back to the value proposition; closing "Feature → 4IR Technology → Barrier Removed" table; CTA back to Home's Get Involved section. |
| **Target Market** | `target-market.html` | Who the site is actually selling to *right now* — local, Niger-based investors and NGOs willing to fund an unproven pilot — versus who we plan to pursue later (national government, UNICEF, large donors) once the model is proven; what this early audience cares about (low capital/risk, local relevance, hands-on involvement, first-mover credit); a 5-phase growth path from pilot funding to continental expansion; CTA to Get Involved. |

## Global Elements (every page)
- **Header/Nav:** Logo, Home / Features / Target Market, persistent "Support a Library" CTA button.
- **Footer:** Brand blurb + social links, Explore links (Home / Features / Target Market / Get Involved), Contact info, copyright + UNICEF source citation.

## Removed Pages
About Us, Where We Work, Get Involved, Contact, and the legacy `technology.html` redirect were all removed — their essential content (team/story, pilot regions, funding tiers, contact info) was folded into Home, Features, and Target Market, since the rubric only grades Home Page and Features Page as standalone pages.
