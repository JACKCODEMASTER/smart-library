## Concept Renderings (CSS/SVG, built in-code — no physical product exists yet)
Located on `features.html`, one per feature pillar:
1. **AI Tutor Lesson Screen** — a tablet-style mockup showing a generated Hausa-language sentence, a new vocabulary word, and a multiple-image answer choice (camel/goat/elephant), labeled "Concept rendering — AI tutor lesson screen."
2. **Student Progress Dashboard** — a mockup of a single child's reading/vocabulary/spelling progress bars plus a machine-learning "level up" recommendation, labeled "Concept rendering — student progress dashboard."
3. **IoT Library Dashboard** — a live-status mockup for "Library Unit #014 — Maradi" showing solar battery %, active tablets, weekly attendance, and security status, labeled "Concept rendering — live IoT dashboard."

These are intentionally labeled as renderings/mockups rather than real screenshots, since the hardware and software are still in the design phase — this keeps the site honest about what currently exists (the organization, the plan, the research) versus what is still being built (the physical product).

## Logo / Icon
An inline SVG mark used in the header and footer of every page: a solar-orange circle (the sun / solar power) with a deep-blue arc-and-rectangle silhouette suggesting a small shelter/shed with a doorway (the library). Chosen over an emoji placeholder for a more professional, consistent, infinitely-scalable brand mark (renders crisply from favicon size up to large footer use).
